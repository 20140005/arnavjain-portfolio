Career Narrative

My Evolution Journey

A chronological trace of my growth from a creative designer to a technical developer and marketing leader.

2024

Prizeman Foils

Creative Design Marketing Internship

The foundation of my professional career. I specialized in translating business goals into visual narratives, managing campaign assets for prizemanfoils.com, and learning the intricacies of client communication within a high-stakes industrial environment.

2025 (Early)

Sunworx Energy

Website Developer

A pivot toward the technical. I took ownership of the digital presence for a leading renewable energy firm, building sunworxenergy.com.au from the ground up. Focused on performance optimization, SEO architecture, and technical data visualizations like the Student Cost Index.

2025 (Late)

ProCare Solar

Head of Marketing

A leadership mandate. Currently orchestrating growth strategies by blending my technical development background with data-driven marketing. Managing end-to-end lead generation and digital transformation.

Next Phase

Future Focus

Product Leadership & Innovation

Moving forward, I am scaling my impact by building AI-integrated tools and platforms that solve real business problems at scale. Combining full-stack engineering with strategic product thinking.
