Career Narrative

Journey: Creative to Technical to Growth

This is a practical timeline of what I worked on, what I learned, and how each stage changed the way I solve problems.

2024

Prizeman Foils

Creative Design Marketing Internship

This was my foundation stage. I worked on visual assets, campaign support, and day-to-day execution tasks. It taught me how professional teams communicate, plan, and deliver under timelines.

2025 (Early)

Sunworx Energy

Website Developer

This was my technical pivot. I focused more on web delivery, website maintenance, performance improvements, and SEO-related structure. It gave me practical experience in converting requirements into production-ready digital work.

2025 (Late)

ProCare Solar

Head of Marketing

In this stage, my scope expanded into planning and leadership. I worked across campaign strategy, content direction, and performance tracking. The biggest shift was learning how to align execution with business outcomes.

Next Phase

Future Focus

Product Leadership & Innovation

Next, I want to keep building at the intersection of product, engineering, and growth. My focus is on creating useful tools with clear impact, not just impressive complexity.
